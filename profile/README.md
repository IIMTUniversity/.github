## 🏛️ About IIMT University

**IIMT University** is a private, multi-disciplinary university established in **2016** in **Meerut, Uttar Pradesh**, under the Uttar Pradesh State University Act. It was founded with one clear purpose: **to bridge the gap between academic education and industry readiness.** The university is part of the IIMT Group of institutions, widely described as North India's largest education group.

Today it offers **100+ career-oriented programmes** at diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral levels, backed by AI-integrated classrooms, innovation and startup labs, and a strong industry network.

| 📌 Quick Facts | |
|---|---|
| 🎓 **Type** | Private University (UGC Recognised) |
| 🏆 **Accreditation** | NAAC **‘A’ Grade** |
| 📅 **Established** | 2016 |
| 📍 **Location** | IIMT Nagar, O Pocket, Ganga Nagar, Mawana Road, Meerut, Uttar Pradesh – 250001 |
| 🌳 **Campus** | 100-acre green campus |
| 🏠 **Hostels** | Wi-Fi enabled hostels for 3,000+ students |
| 🏥 **On Campus** | Hospital, digital library, gymnasium, multi-sport facilities, auditorium, cafeteria |
| 🌐 **Website** | [iimtu.edu.in](https://iimtu.edu.in/) |
